## Early design risks

- **Cognitive inhibition remains the skill.** Explain “相手に負ける手” once, demonstrate the three relations in unrestricted practice, then keep rock-left/scissors-middle/paper-right across illustration, hiragana, text and Reflex. Identical color treatment must not encode the answer. Difficulty should come from converting the displayed opponent, not searching shuffled positions or decoding unclear hand artwork.
- **The timer begins with the usable cue.** Opponent image must be decoded/visible before the first answer deadline starts. All initial30 answers have a full2.0s; Reflex remains0.8s. Feedback/phase animation must not conceal part of the next cue or use its answer clock.
- **READ is genuinely untimed.** Let the player interpret the sentence before starting2.0s. Continue key/pointer release must not itself answer scissors, and held/repeated input must not spill into a new question. Hidden Continue/transition/header focus must not swallow the first real answer; preserve previous011/015 focus lessons.
- **Presentation changes are comprehensible.** Short10/20/30 transitions should introduce the next format without a long wait or revealing every later surprise in the first explanation. Text questions require unique plain meanings, not a Japanese-language riddle whose ambiguity causes failure.
- **Positive failure inversion.** Correct LOSE should be brief, affirmative and visually distinguishable from accidental win/draw/timeout. Results should name the ordinary opponent/player relation as well as what losing hand was needed. A funny comment must not replace actionable feedback or demean the player.
- **Incentive remains readable.** Primary SCORE, cumulative losses and Reflex streak should agree with phase points. At30 correct, score5000; Reflex speed bands must be observable as modest bonuses, not a hidden contradictory scoring rule. Losing once ends the run and immediate retry should feel available.
- **Phone is an equivalent decision.** Three actual native pads at fixed positions with useful size/spacing, cue/timer always visible in portrait. Simultaneous contacts/held keys must not credit multiple questions. Physical thumb comfort and real-device latency are separate human tests.
