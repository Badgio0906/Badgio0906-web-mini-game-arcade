# Game018 gameplay model handoff

Model-only implementation; no browser/UI/publication/human-feel claims.

## Files and public contract

- `types.ts`: phase, shoe, score, effect and obstacle types.
- `shoes.ts`: five immutable shoe definitions.
- `physics.ts`: normalized bounded input, actual fixed-step integration, swept segment/box collision, deterministic stream and trajectory interpolation.
- `ShoeRun.ts`: native timestamp stop/pause and four isolated practice stages.
- `game018.test.ts`: meaningful timing, trajectory, score, category, collision and practice tests. Imports must be converted from `./...` to `../../src/games/game018/...` on integration.

The UI calls `start(now, shoeType)`, `startPractice(now, stage, shoeType)`, `settle(now)`, `stop(now)`, `pause(value, now)` and reads the current public fields. `snapshot()` deep-copies result, effects, obstacles and locked fields for read-only DEV diagnostics. No preset-input setter exists in the live run. Exported `simulate(inputs)` is used by isolated pure tests, never to override native browser timing.

`angle`, `spin`, `power` show current oscillators in their input phase, then remain locked. `locked` explicitly records the three committed values. `phaseElapsed`, `phaseProgress`, `flightElapsed`, `flightDuration`, `time` support render/QA clocks. World `position.y` is positive upwards. `rotation` and `angularVelocity` are radians. `breaks` means actual total ground-object penetrations; `breakCombo` counts consecutive penetrations and resets on BONK. `maxBreakCombo` is retained for the result. The final result and TOTAL remain null until landing completes.

## Timing and practice

ANGLE is a smooth 2.8-second5°–85° round trip. SPIN is a1.65-second signed sinusoid. POWER is a1.36-second cycle with a100-millisecond flat peak plus near-peak JUST MAX edges; JUST MAX threshold99.5. Each angle/spin lock holds300ms; JUST MAX holds380ms; kick600ms; landing650ms. Comparisons tolerate1e-9seconds, avoiding representational misses at exact native timestamps.

Practice stages0/1/2 stop the isolated angle/spin/power then show explicit practice-complete. Stage3 follows real three-input launch/flight/landing and keeps every score component zero. No timer expires individual practice input. UI owns the explicit Next/retry action and real-score/storage/telemetry isolation.

## Physics and categories

Fixed1/30s semi-implicit integration uses velocity-dependent drag, gravity/weight, shoe aerodynamics, signed angular velocity, spin stability and spin lift. Every crossed obstacle is checked with a complete2D segment/box slab intersection, including roof entry and high-speed tunnelling. Low-route high-power throws may bounce at most3times. Launch velocity is capped3600m/s and the supported launch set lands within a bounded120s physical horizon. The full physically integrated trajectory is replayed with interpolation at cinematic time: ordinary flight12–24s, low-power4–9s. Replay changes time scale only, never ending coordinates or collision outcomes.

The deterministic grid contained1,890 combinations, all finite/ground-landed, maximum1,730 actual samples. Ground objects are generated by nearby world cell; at most24 are exposed to Canvas, actual impact history64 and recent effects18. Objects contain no people.

SNEAKER is strongest ordinary balanced distance; PAPER gains height but suffers extra horizontal drag and weak penetration; ZORI has the strongest spin rate/bonus and stabilizing efficiency; LEATHER penetrates tough low-route objects; IRON GETA is poor on ordinary power but gets exceptional JUST MAX speed only at angle15°–28° or48°–68°, with spin magnitude.65–.94. Iron outside that window still gains a smaller MAX bonus.

Actual ascent crossings trigger CLOUD NINE550m, AIRPLANE BREAK2200m with power>=85, UFO INCIDENT6500m with power>=97, ORBITAL SHOE25000m with JUST MAX. These sky penetrations are special-event bonuses, not ground BREAK count. Secret combinations: JET STREAM, TORNADO ZORI, BUSINESS MISSILE, IRON BREAKER / IRON GETA EVENT. DRILL THROUGH needs3consecutive ground penetrations, low angle, high spin and power. HIGHWAY STAR needs angle<=12° and power>=85.

## Score and limits

Distance floor(meters×4), height floor(maxMeters×2), ground break1200each, integrated-rotation bonus up to10000 (ZORI125per turn, others80), JUST MAX2500, unique event1000 except ORBITAL10000 / IRON5000. All components sum to TOTAL; final distance is exactly the final integrated landing x. Distances display meters below1000 and kilometers above.

The physical world distances and orbital threshold are comic game units; they do not claim real-world flight accuracy. Human timing/visual/fun review remains separate from these model tests and cloud-machine performance timings.
